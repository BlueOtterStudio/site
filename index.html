06 — Questions

Before you download.

The short answers. Everything else is in the app — free to try on iOS and Android.

Is Orange free?

Orange is free to download on iOS and Android, and the built-in HSK 1–6 decks come included — so you can start studying right away.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Start at HSK 1 with tones, first characters and the most common words. The app builds from there, one small step at a time.

Which devices does it work on?

Orange runs on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. Download it from the App Store or Google Play using the buttons on this page.

How much time do I need each day?

Five focused minutes keeps your reviews moving. Short daily sessions beat weekend cramming — the scheduler is built around exactly that.

Can I add my own words and decks?

Yes. Scan a page with the photo reader or add words manually. They join the same spaced-repetition schedule as the built-in HSK decks.

Does it replace a teacher?

It's a study tool, not a classroom. It handles vocabulary, stroke order, listening and instant grammar questions — brilliant alongside a class, and more than enough on its own.
